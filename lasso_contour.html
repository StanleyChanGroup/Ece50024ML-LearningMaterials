What you're seeing

The red ellipses are level curves of the squared loss (x−1)²+(y−1)² — circles centred at the target (1,1). The dashed blue diamond is the ℓ₁ ball of radius r = λ/2. The purple dot is the LASSO solution — where the expanding diamond first touches a loss ellipse.

Because the target (1,1) lies on the diagonal and the bowl is perfectly symmetric, the solution travels along the diagonal and both coordinates shrink at the same rate. Sparsity (one coordinate exactly zero) only appears when λ ≥ 2.

Key insight: The diamond's corners sit on the axes. A generic (asymmetric) target would cause the diamond to hit a corner first → sparse solution. The symmetric target (1,1) is a special case where both coordinates zero out simultaneously.
