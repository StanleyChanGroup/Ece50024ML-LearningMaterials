What Exercise 6 actually proves

Parts (a) and (b) together show: any two minimizers θ, β must satisfy Aθ = Aβ and ‖θ‖₁ = ‖β‖₁. The proof never claims θ = β as vectors — only that their predictions and ℓ₁ norms match.

The non-unique case (right panel) is fully consistent: every point on the orange segment satisfies both conditions. The "contradiction" in the proof only fires when Aθ ≠ Aβ, which never happens here.
