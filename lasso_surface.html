Orange segment = flat valley where all solutions live

Why the valley is flat

Degenerate case (A=[1,1])Loss = (x+y−1)² + λ(|x|+|y|). Along the solution segment both terms are constant → perfectly flat valley → infinitely many minimizers.
Full-rank case (A=I)Loss = (x−1)²+(y−1)²+λ(|x|+|y|). Strictly curved in every direction → single point at the bottom → unique minimizer.
